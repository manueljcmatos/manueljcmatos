# Inkstone — learn Mandarin from the roots up

A single-file web app for learning Mandarin by understanding it: what each character means, where it came from, and how to use it in real conversations.

Open `index.html` in any browser (phone or desktop). No install, no build step.

## Sections

- **学 Learn** – character of the day, how Chinese works, and a learning path
- **字 Characters** – 61 core characters with origin, radical, strokes, fun facts, example words and a tracing pad
- **说 Speak** – the four tones (with pitch contours), survival phrases with word-for-word meanings, and role-play conversations
- **亚 Asia** – Asian countries through their Chinese names, chengyu idioms and their stories, cultural etiquette
- **练 Practice** – flashcards, meaning quiz, tone quiz (progress saved in your browser)

Every Chinese character in the app is tappable, and its underline colour shows its tone. Audio uses your device's built-in Chinese (zh-CN) voice.
